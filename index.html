Ninefold

A free collection tracker for the Riftbound trading card game.

Ninefold lets you browse every set, track the cards you own and how many copies you have, organise them into binders, watch your set completion climb, keep an eye on prices, and add cards in seconds by scanning them with your camera.

In development for iPhone
